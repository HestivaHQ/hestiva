# Homent Moment brand language

The Homent Moment is the customer-facing emotional idea behind the Homent brand. Homent is the company, thoughtful home cleaning is the service, and the Homent Moment is the outcome: returning to a beautifully cared-for home with more time for what matters.

## Usage principles

- Use `Homent Moment` selectively so it remains distinctive.
- Keep service names, local SEO headings, factual service descriptions, legal copy and functional form labels clear and literal.
- Use the concept most strongly in the homepage hero, the end of the cleaning journey, the brand-story section and the main conversion CTA.
- Reinforce the idea elsewhere through language about time, calm, home, living, returning and moments that matter rather than repeating the phrase in every section.
- Do not rename Homent, service products, navigation items or internal architecture around the phrase.

## Canonical idea

A Homent Moment is the feeling of returning to a home that has been thoughtfully cared for, when the cleaning is handled and your time is yours again.

## Homepage language

- Hero: `Come home to your Homent Moment.`
- Hero support connects thoughtful cleaning to a cared-for home and more time for the moments that matter.
- Process step 03 becomes `Enjoy your Homent Moment` as the outcome of the service journey.
- The About section defines the phrase in plain language rather than leaving it as an unexplained slogan.

## Boundaries

This language pass does not alter pricing, quote logic, service scope, SEO architecture, structured data, canonical URLs, form behaviour, email routing, deployment or the Website/HestivaOS boundary.
