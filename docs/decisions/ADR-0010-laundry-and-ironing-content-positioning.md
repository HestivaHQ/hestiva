# ADR-0010: Laundry and ironing content positioning

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-14

## Context

Laundry and Ironing are operational add-ons under the Issue #79 model, but useful public website content about them should remain available for customer education and search visibility.

## Decision

The website may retain a dedicated public content card/page for this capability, presented as **Laundry & Ironing Add-On** rather than as a standalone bookable service.

The quote flow must not offer Laundry or Ironing as a primary service. Laundry and Ironing remain available only with eligible cleaning visits, with facilities and requested load quantities captured through the structured add-on model.

The existing `laundry-folding` URL slug may be retained for compatibility, provided the customer-facing content clearly reflects the current add-on-only operating model.

## Consequences

Public content and imagery can be retained without creating a conflicting commercial service model. Customer-facing copy, service navigation and structured quote transport must consistently distinguish informational content from bookable primary services.
